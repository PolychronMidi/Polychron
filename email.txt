Subject: myth0s Update -- The "Audit Protocol" External System and Its Integration Path

Hi,

Thank you for the warm reception of the last update. As promised, this note
reports on a discovered external system we have been studying for integration
into myth0s. Internally we have been tracking it as the "Audit Protocol." It
arrived as a literature-novelty audit specification, but our study concluded its
real value is not literature-specific at all: it is a portable, anti-sycophancy
adversarial-honesty grammar that maps almost directly onto the coherence problems
myth0s exists to solve.

Executive summary: the Audit Protocol is a calibrated method for judging claims
without inflating them. It forces decomposition before judgment, demands both
supporting and contradictory evidence for every claim, scores how thorough the
audit actually was, weights claims by importance, and applies an explicit
downward calibration step to counter the well-documented tendency of LLM judges
to over-rate. We have studied it specifically as a candidate review discipline
for the myth0s mesh, and we recommend integrating its grammar (not its
literature-specific scoring) into our reviewer charter and Context Capsules.

What the system is

The Audit Protocol is, in plain terms, a discipline for hypothesis-death. Its
central stance is that killing a weak claim is the achievement, not a failure.
It operationalizes that stance with a fixed sequence: strip author identity to
reduce sycophancy, decompose the subject into discrete numbered claims, audit
each claim against the most relevant prior work, record what supports it AND what
was considered but does not subsume it, then aggregate into a calibrated score
with an explicit "consider lowering this by one tier" check. It also recommends a
second, different-model audit and flags "audit-uncertain" when the two diverge.

We were drawn to it because these are exactly the controls myth0s needs. Our
mesh's biggest risk is not that peers cannot find issues; it is that powerful
forked peers, eager to be useful, manufacture or inflate findings. The Audit
Protocol is a ready-made antidote to that exact failure mode, and it arrives with
its own hygiene rules that forbid special-pleading, forbid softening high
subsumption, and default every uncertain score to the lower value.

Findings from our study -- what we will port into myth0s

We assessed the system the same way we assess any candidate substrate: keep what
generalizes, reject what does not, and verify before adopting.

Generalizable lessons we will integrate:

1. Finding-death is progress. The single most valuable line in the system is its
   framing that a low score is a successful audit, not a failed subject. Ported
   to myth0s: a peer that correctly reports "no decision-changing issue found,
   here is the evidence" has succeeded. This directly counters reviewer inflation
   and protects the credibility of every P0/P1 the mesh does raise.

2. Decompose before judging. The protocol extracts discrete, independently
   checkable claims before scoring anything. Ported to myth0s: every
   decision-changing finding decomposes into claim, affected artifact/function,
   failure mode, precondition, evidence, counter-evidence, one-line fix,
   severity, and confidence. This sharpens our already-good "finding + fix"
   output into an auditable schema.

3. Require contradictory evidence. This is the highest-leverage import. The
   protocol mandates both a supporting-evidence and a contradictory-evidence
   section for every claim. Ported to myth0s: for every claimed defect, the peer
   must cite at least one existing guard, test, or code path that might already
   cover it, and explain why it does or does not. This is a structural
   false-positive reducer.

4. Score audit thoroughness explicitly. The protocol separates "how novel" from
   "how hard did you actually look." Ported to myth0s: a severity rating carries
   a separate thoroughness score (source-read only, source plus adjacent
   tests/config, or source plus runtime reproduction/invariant check), so we can
   distinguish a plausible P1 from a proven one.

5. Weight by importance, normalized. The protocol weights load-bearing claims
   above peripheral ones. Ported to myth0s: findings carry a decision-impact
   weight, so peer budget is spent on load-bearing issues instead of cosmetic
   noise.

6. Anti-inflation calibration. The protocol assumes the judge over-rates and
   defaults downward near every boundary. Ported to myth0s with one safety
   caveat: when uncertain between two severities, default to the lower UNLESS
   there is executable evidence or a fail-open safety consequence, in which case
   we still fail closed. This protects against mesh theatrics without weakening
   real safety gates.

7. Cross-family disagreement as an uncertainty signal. The protocol runs a
   second audit from a different model family and reports "audit-uncertain" on
   divergence. Ported to myth0s: for high-impact architectural or security
   claims, red/blue/purple divergence beyond one severity tier becomes an
   explicit "audit-uncertain, recommend human review" artifact. The useful output
   is not forced consensus; it is calibrated dissent.

What we deliberately will NOT import:

- The literature-specific novelty calculus (component/synthesis/application/
  methodology novelty tiers) does not map to code review. We will translate the
  grammar into our own axes: component validity, systemic impact, exploitability/
  failure reach, and remediation confidence.
- The full prior-art literature search is expensive and only warranted for
  research-grade or budget-justifying claims. We will trigger it tier-by-tier,
  not on every routine review.
- The system's bibliography will be treated as unverified until we check it. We
  adopt the method immediately; we do not treat its citations as canonical
  doctrine until sourced.

Why this matters to the R&D case

This is the kind of discovery that compounds the existing investment. myth0s
already preserves context, grounds reviews in bounded capsules, and routes
adversarial peers through hard guardrails. The Audit Protocol gives us the
missing piece: a calibrated honesty layer that makes the mesh's verdicts
trustworthy enough to act on at scale. A self-improving review system is only as
valuable as its resistance to its own inflation, and this system is a proven
operationalization of exactly that resistance.

Integrating it does not require new infrastructure. It is a discipline we layer
onto the reviewer charter and the high-value Context Capsule template -- low cost,
high leverage, and directly aligned with enterprise trust requirements around
auditable, self-correcting agent systems.

Forward trajectory

The next phase folds this discovery into the broader myth0s roadmap:

1. Integrate the Audit Protocol grammar into the reviewer charter and a new
   high-impact Context Capsule "claim-audit addendum" -- contradictory-evidence
   requirement, thoroughness score, decision-impact weight, anti-inflation
   calibration, and finding-death framing -- without adding ceremony to routine
   reviews.
2. Broaden target coverage: keep pointing the mesh at fresh load-bearing surfaces
   (pre-write gates, state registry, stop-chain policy, session-state lifecycle,
   transcript compaction, proxy request mutation, tool-result semantics).
3. Build a formal evaluation harness: single reviewer vs red/blue/purple mesh,
   scored on unique catches, false-positive reduction, cost, and regression
   impact -- now with the calibration layer as a measurable variable.
4. Improve cost controls without neutering capability: smarter target selection,
   capsule-evidence caching, adaptive effort, and per-finding cost telemetry.
5. Productize Context Capsules and the claim-audit addendum as reusable Anthropic
   review primitives.
6. Expand policy and governance integration so every autonomous action stays
   governable and auditable at the control plane.
7. Prepare an internal supervised pilot with success criteria: measurable unique
   defect discovery, low false-positive burden, stable cost envelope, calibrated
   dissent surfaced, and zero policy-bypass regressions.

Recommended upcoming investment remains three lanes -- core substrate
engineering, evaluation/observability, and productization research -- now with
the Audit Protocol integration as a concrete, near-term, low-cost deliverable in
the first and third lanes.

In short: we discovered a calibrated anti-inflation audit system, studied it
against the myth0s coherence thesis, and found it is the honesty layer the mesh
was missing. The detailed phase plan is going to you and the review board
separately for approval.
